Biography

Rongtian Fu is currently a Ph.D. student at the Parallel Computing and Intelligent Systems Laboratory (ParCIS Lab), Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), under the supervision of Prof. Shigang Li, starting from 2024. Prior to that, he received his B.S. degree in Computer Science from BUPT in 2024.
